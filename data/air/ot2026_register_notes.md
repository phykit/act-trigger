The supremecourt.gov docket caption says only "Suncor Energy (U.S.A.) Inc., et al.", and the Rule 29.6 statement in the petition was not read.

- **Paramount (25-459).** The PSKY parent link rests on Paramount Skydance's Q3 2025 10-Q. Later changes in the group's structure or listing (for example from any subsequent M&A), and whether 247Sports is still owned by the group, were not checked.

The tickers INTC (respondents' merits brief), MDU (10-K cover), SU (AIF), PSKY (10-Q cover), RGR (10-K cover) and SWBI (10-K, Nasdaq Global Select; ticker taken from the filing name) were confirmed from the documents themselves.

### Category (c) judgement calls

- **Included: 25-238 / 25-566 (Viramontes v. Cook County; Grant v. Higgins).** Category (c) is used because Connecticut, through its DESPP Commissioner, is a respondent. Cook County is a local government, not a state. Treating the effect on firearms makers (RGR, SWBI) as direct and material is a judgement: the holding decides whether semiautomatic-rifle bans can stand in the jurisdictions that have them. The petitioners moved on 29 Oct 2026 to dismiss petitioner Cutberto Viramontes under Rule 46.2; the case continues with the other petitioners.
- **Excluded: 25-966 DOL v. Sun Valley Orchards.** The questions are whether Article III and 8 U.S.C. 1188(g)(2) permit the Department of Labor to adjudicate monetary remedies against H-2A employers. The effect on listed agricultural employers was judged too diffuse to name exposed tickers. The case has wider relevance to agency in-house adjudication generally.
- **Excluded: 26-204 DOJ v. McNutt.** This concerns the 1868 federal ban on home distilling; the Fifth Circuit held it unconstitutional. It was judged immaterial to listed distillers.
- **Excluded: 26-426 DHS v. D.V.D. and 25-1223 Montoya Palacios v. Liggins.** These are immigration removal and detention-fee questions; any effect on listed detention operators was judged indirect.
- **Not entered as category (c): 25-159 Hoffmann and 25-170 Suncor.** Neither case has a federal-agency or state party (the United States is amicus only). The wider industry relevance (interstate gas pipelines; oil and gas producers facing climate suits) is described in the industry_note column of the existing (a)/(b) rows, without adding tickers.
