# Earth v2, amendment 2: the lead party on each side of a Federal Circuit ruling

Registered 31 October 2026, before any run under Earth v2. It amends the last sentence of item 3 of `earth_v2_prereg.md` (SHA-256 f31acf8b9450f8bb24251d37e5e99addf30e1ec838030dc8aabfd611f644f510) as amended by `earth_v2_amendment_1.md` (SHA-256 7b9bc67ab1acd937a76207c2230690e7a39b45ac1250a44332acd62af4c3cfee); everything else stands.

## Reason

Item 3 takes a side's "first name" (used by Wood and Storm for their search terms, and in the universe's petitioner and respondent columns) to be the first party in GovInfo's order for that side. The built list showed that GovInfo's order does not follow the caption. In U.S. Water Services v. Novozymes, the first appellant listed is an individual, Roy Johnson, not U.S. Water Services. The primary arm is unaffected, because it matches every named party.

## Change

A side's lead is the first party on that side, in GovInfo's order, whose normalised name (Earth item 3) begins with the same word as either side of GovInfo's title for the ruling. If no party on the side qualifies, the lead is the first party on that side in GovInfo's order. The lead is listed first on its side, so a rule that takes the first matched listed company on a side (Death) starts from it.
